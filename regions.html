A continent of flavor

Explore the culinary regions of Africa

Climate, history, migration, and community all shape the ingredients and techniques found across Africa's kitchens.

01 / West Africa

Bold, vibrant, and deeply communal

West African cooking brings together grains, legumes, seafood, and vegetables with powerful aromatics. Peppers, tomatoes, and palm oil create the foundations of many beloved dishes.

Slow simmering and one-pot cooking make meals generous enough for family celebrations and everyday gatherings.

Try Jollof Rice

02 / North Africa

Fragrant spices and patient cooking

North African cuisine reflects centuries of trade and cultural exchange. Cumin, coriander, cinnamon, and preserved lemon bring warmth and brightness to dishes from coastlines to deserts.

Tagines, couscous, and flatbreads show how careful layering of flavor turns simple ingredients into memorable meals.

Try Moroccan Tagine

03 / East Africa

Ancient grains and shared plates

East African food traditions celebrate grains, greens, pulses, and aromatic stews. Teff, fermentation, and berbere spice are central to many Ethiopian and Eritrean meals.

Food is often served communally, encouraging conversation and connection around one shared platter.

Try Injera
