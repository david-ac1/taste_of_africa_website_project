Our story

Every meal is a doorway to heritage.

Tastes of Africa celebrates the people, places, and traditions behind the continent's extraordinary food cultures.

Our mission

Share the story behind every dish.

We believe food is more than what is on the plate. It carries memory, identity, hospitality, and knowledge passed from one generation to the next.

This project offers an accessible introduction to African cuisine while honoring its diversity and encouraging curious cooks to learn, share, and gather.

A living history

Connected by ingredients and imagination

African cuisine has been shaped by local harvests, ancient trade routes, migration, and adaptation. Grains such as millet and teff, root vegetables, leafy greens, legumes, spices, and seafood all tell different stories about the communities that use them.

From home kitchens to restaurants around the world, African dishes continue to evolve while keeping their sense of place. Exploring these recipes is an invitation to appreciate both their history and their future.

A place to visit

Explore the Zeitz MOCAA in Cape Town

African creativity is celebrated in museums, markets, restaurants, and homes across the continent. The Zeitz Museum of Contemporary Art Africa is one landmark connecting visitors with contemporary African stories.
