Bunny Chow

Born in Durban, Bunny Chow is a generous loaf filled with spicy curry and enjoyed as a satisfying street-food meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 round unsliced loaf of bread
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 500 g potatoes and carrots
  • 1 onion and 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons curry powder
  • 1 cup vegetable stock

Method

View cooking instructions
  1. Fry the onion, tomatoes, and curry powder until fragrant.
  2. Add the vegetables and stock, then simmer until tender.
  3. Cut a square from the loaf and hollow out the center.
  4. Fill the bread with curry and serve with the removed piece for dipping.
