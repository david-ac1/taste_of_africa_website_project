Central Africa

Isombe

Isombe transforms cassava leaves into a richly textured stew, often served with plantains, rice, or other comforting staples.

Ingredients

  • 500 g prepared cassava leaves
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 2 cups vegetable stock
  • Salt and chili to taste

Method

View cooking instructions
  1. Cook the prepared leaves in stock until soft and tender.
  2. Add onion, tomatoes, salt, and chili, then simmer gently.
  3. Stir in the peanut butter until the stew is creamy.
  4. Continue cooking until the flavors come together, then serve warm.
