Moroccan Tagine

Named for the conical vessel in which it cooks, tagine brings together slow-cooked vegetables, aromatic spices, and tender meat or legumes.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 carrots and 2 potatoes, chopped
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 1 teaspoon cumin and ground coriander
  • 1 preserved lemon, sliced

Method

View cooking instructions
  1. Warm the oil and soften the onion with the spices.
  2. Add the vegetables, chickpeas, and enough stock to almost cover.
  3. Cover and simmer gently until the vegetables are tender.
  4. Finish with preserved lemon and fresh herbs.
