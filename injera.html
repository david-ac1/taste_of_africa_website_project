East Africa

Injera

This soft, tangy flatbread is made with teff flour and fermentation. It doubles as both plate and utensil for shared Ethiopian meals.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups teff flour
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups warm water
  • 1 teaspoon active dry yeast
  • Pinch of salt

Method

View cooking instructions
  1. Whisk the flours, water, and yeast into a smooth batter.
  2. Cover and ferment at room temperature for one to three days.
  3. Stir in salt, then pour a thin layer into a hot non-stick pan.
  4. Cover until the surface is set and full of small holes. Do not flip.
