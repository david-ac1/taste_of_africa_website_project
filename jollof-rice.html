Jollof Rice

A vibrant one-pot rice dish simmered in a rich tomato and pepper base, Jollof Rice is a centerpiece of celebrations across West Africa.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 ripe tomatoes, blended
  • 2 red bell peppers, blended
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon thyme and curry powder

Method

View cooking instructions
  1. Cook the onion in oil until soft, then add the blended tomato and pepper mixture.
  2. Season and simmer until the sauce thickens and the oil rises to the top.
  3. Stir in the rinsed rice, cover, and cook gently until tender.
  4. Rest for ten minutes before fluffing and serving.
